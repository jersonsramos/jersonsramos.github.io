# Portfolio design system

## Direction

Editorial Product Casebook: a dark ink workspace with a warm paper casework column and forest/sage signals. The page treats the portfolio as a working product system rather than a résumé document.

## Layout

- Three-column desktop scan: identity and contact, experience evidence, project index.
- Mobile stacks identity, experience, then projects without hiding project exploration.
- Employers can jump directly to Experience, Projects, or Contact from the compact scan navigation.

## Visual language

- Ink background: `#101110` with raised surfaces in `#171917` and `#20231f`.
- Warm paper casework: `#f1f0ea` with dark ink text `#101110`.
- Signal accent: lime `#b8f27a`, supported by forest `#235f45` and sage `#8dcf74`.
- Borders are quiet, 1px, and used to define columns and evidence groups.
- Syne carries identity and display moments; IBM Plex Sans carries the readable evidence layer.

## Interaction

- The architecture → build → production signal is the signature motion moment.
- Project galleries expose screenshot lightboxes with keyboard focus management.
- Dense contribution evidence is progressively disclosed with accessible details controls.
- Experience and gallery progress indicators make long evidence surfaces legible.

## Accessibility and performance

- Visible focus styles, keyboard-friendly controls, useful image alternatives, and reduced-motion fallbacks are required.
- The site remains a single static HTML/CSS/JavaScript page with no runtime backend or framework dependency.
- Existing claims, links, screenshots, and ownership boundaries are treated as source truth.
