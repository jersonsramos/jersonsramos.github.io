# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript for GitHub Pages

## Users

Primary users are employers and hiring managers evaluating Jerson Ramos for full-stack product engineering roles. Potential freelance clients are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio communicates Jerson's experience building and contributing to SaaS platforms, business applications, operational workflows, payments, automation, and production web products. Success means an employer can understand his role and strongest evidence quickly, explore relevant projects, and contact him.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents a product-engineering perspective across the full path from discovery and architecture through implementation, testing, deployment preparation, and ongoing product support, while distinguishing built work from contributed work.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through a public GitHub Pages site and scan the page quickly on desktop or mobile. They may inspect project links, project screenshots, experience evidence, technology context, and contact options.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a single static page with no backend or framework dependency.
- Project exploration should be as prominent as experience review.
- The page must remain responsive and keyboard accessible.
- Existing portfolio claims, project descriptions, links, and evidence boundaries must be preserved unless explicitly corrected by the user.
- Saving or editing the page does not publish it; GitHub Pages upload remains a separate user action.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jerson Ramos.
- Role: Full-Stack Product Engineer.
- Existing visual direction: editorial ink, warm paper, forest/sage green, and a restrained signal accent.
- Voice: direct, credible, evidence-grounded, and understandable to non-specialist employers.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing experience and project copy in `index.html`.
- Existing project screenshots under `images/`.
- Existing profile photograph `jerson-ramos.jpg`.
- Existing external project links and contact links in `index.html`.
- No unsupported testimonials, customer results, revenue claims, or credentials should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the employer's scan path obvious within the first viewport.
- Give project exploration equal prominence to employment history.
- Lead with business outcomes and contribution scope before technical detail.
- Preserve evidence boundaries between personally built and contributed work.
- Keep the experience lightweight, static, responsive, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, touch-sized controls, useful image alternatives, and clear content hierarchy on small screens.
